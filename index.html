WHAT IT DOES

Three things, done properly

Every window, one calendar

FGN Savings Bonds, Treasury Bills, FGN Bonds, Sukuk, green bonds and share offers — each with the rate, the minimum, how to take part, and the official source it came from. Dates that have not been published yet are clearly marked “Expected”.

Told before it closes

Set an alert and you are reminded two days before a window opens, on the morning it opens, and — for windows that run several days — two days before it shuts. That last one is the one that rescues most people.

Explained in plain English

Nineteen short lessons, every example in naira. Why your savings are shrinking, how to spot a scheme, what tax actually takes, and what each thing you can buy really pays.

THE LESSONS

From “what is inflation” to “how do I actually buy this”

Two stages. The first covers why any of this matters and how not to be cheated. The second goes through every instrument you can actually buy in Nigeria — what it is, how it hands you money, what tax comes off, and what happens if you need your money back early.

BEFORE YOU ASK

Yield never touches your money

It is not a broker

Yield tells you when windows open and explains what they are. You invest through your own bank, a stockbroker, or an SEC-registered app — never through us. We never ask for your bank details or your BVN.

It is not advice

Everything here is education and information. Nobody at Yield knows your income, your debts or your goals, so nothing here is a recommendation. Always confirm dates and rates with the official source before you act.

It is not the government

Yield is independent, and not affiliated with the DMO, the CBN, the NBS or any other government body. We compile the calendar from their published circulars and link to them so you can check.
