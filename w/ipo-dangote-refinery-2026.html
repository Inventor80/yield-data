Dangote Refinery share offer (IPO)

14 September 2026 to 13 October 2026

The refinery is selling shares to the public for the first time — the first refinery ever offered on the Nigerian Exchange. Buying shares makes you part-owner of the business, so what you get back depends on how it performs. Unlike a bond, nothing is guaranteed.

RATE
₦525 per share
MINIMUM
10 shares — ₦5,250
TENOR
No end date — you hold the shares until you sell them

How to take part

  1. Applications close on Tuesday 13 October — this is the last week
  2. You need a CSCS account, which your broker or investment app opens for you
  3. Subscribe through NGX Invest, a licensed stockbroker, or an SEC-registered investment app
  4. Check any platform on the SEC register at sec.gov.ng before you send money
  5. If the offer is oversubscribed you receive fewer shares than you applied for, and the rest is refunded
  6. Allotment and the listing date follow the timetable in the prospectus, and are announced after the close

Source: Nigerian Exchange Group (NGX) — official page. Checked 8 October 2026.
