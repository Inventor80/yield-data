Treasury Bill auction — 21 October

21 October 2026

Short-term government borrowing, in 91, 182 and 364-day bills. You are not paid interest: you buy the bill for less than it repays. A 364-day bill clearing at 15.89%, September’s rate, means paying about ₦86,300 today for ₦100,000 in a year — the ₦13,700 gap is your return, less 10% withholding tax.

RATE
Set at auction — the 364-day bill cleared at 15.89% on 23 September
MINIMUM
₦50,000,000 at the auction · from about ₦100,000 through a bank or broker
TENOR
91, 182 or 364 days
The CBN auctions Treasury Bills on Wednesdays, roughly twice a month. Its Q4 2026 issuance calendar had still not been published when this was checked — the first Q4 auction ran on 7 October — so this date is an estimate. Confirm on cbn.gov.ng.

How to take part

  1. The auction itself is for large institutions. To buy a smaller parcel, ask your bank’s treasury desk or a stockbroker
  2. Ask what rate they are offering and compare it with the auction rate — the gap is their margin
  3. Since October 2025, 10% withholding tax is deducted from the return on Treasury Bills. FGN bonds are still exempt
  4. Your money itself is returned in full at maturity

Source: Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) — official page. Checked 8 October 2026.
