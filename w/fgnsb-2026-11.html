FGN Savings Bond — November offer

2 November 2026 to 6 November 2026

The government borrows from ordinary savers for two or three years and pays you a fixed rate every quarter. September paid 15.12% on the 3-year: on ₦100,000 that is ₦3,780 landing in your account every three months, then your ₦100,000 back at the end.

RATE
Set in the November circular
MINIMUM
₦5,000
TENOR
2 or 3 years
The DMO opens this window in the first full week of each month. The circular for this month had not been published when this was checked — confirm the dates and the rate before acting.

How to take part

  1. Watch dmo.gov.ng for the November circular in the first week
  2. Contact a DMO-appointed stockbroker or your bank’s investment desk before it opens
  3. You need a bank account in your own name, your BVN and a valid ID
  4. The window runs Monday to Friday — five working days, no extensions

Source: Debt Management Office (DMO) — official page. Checked 29 September 2026.
